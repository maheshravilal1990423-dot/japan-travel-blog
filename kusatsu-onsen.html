Kusatsu Onsen: A relaxing hot-spring escape

Kusatsu is a famous hot-spring town in Gunma Prefecture, known for its steaming Yubatake area and traditional onsen atmosphere.

What to see

Start around Yubatake, the central hot-spring landmark, then explore the nearby streets, small shops and footbath areas. Check seasonal conditions before travelling, especially in winter.
